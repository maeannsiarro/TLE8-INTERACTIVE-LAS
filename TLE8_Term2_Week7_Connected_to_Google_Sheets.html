Learning competency
Discuss the Concepts in Making Garments.
Learning objectives
  1. Classify various types of clothing based on their styles, purposes, and occasions;
  2. Identify sewing supplies and materials commonly used in garment production;
  3. Examine different types of fabric and their characteristics by creating a fabric swatch book; and
  4. Appreciate the importance of understanding the concepts in making garments.
Content focus
Introduction to garments; types of garments; trends and innovation; ergonomics; supplies and materials used for making garments; kinds of fabric; finishing materials.

Day 4 • Performance task

Build a textile swatch book

Learning competency: Discuss the Concepts in Making Garments.
Objectives
  • Examine different types of fabric and their characteristics.
  • Collect, identify, and describe fabric samples.
  • Share observations about the fabrics and their possible uses.
Instructions: Create a Textile Swatch Book by collecting different fabric swatches. Examine each sample closely and record its name (if known), texture, thickness, appearance, fiber or fabric category (if known), and possible use. Include at least five samples. You may attach clean fabric scraps, or draw and describe them if you cannot obtain swatches. Ask permission before taking fabric from clothing. Share your swatch book with your class or teacher.
